How to use

  1. Drag a photo to where it should go. An orange line shows where it will land.
  2. Or click a photo, then shift-click the photo it should go in front of.
  3. Click a photo to turn it, remove it, or keep it on screen longer.
  4. Double-click a photo to see it big.
  5. Choose the look of the video under Settings.
  6. Press Build video. It saves your changes first, then makes the video.
